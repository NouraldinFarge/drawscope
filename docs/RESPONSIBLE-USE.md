# Responsible use

Lottery drawings are designed to be random and independent under their stated rules.

- Historical frequency does not guarantee future frequency.
- A number being overdue does not make it due.
- Hot and cold labels describe a selected historical sample only.
- Experimental weights are not official probabilities.
- Valid combinations generally have equal theoretical probability in a fair drawing.
- Backtesting can be distorted by overfitting and leakage.
- Statistical anomalies do not prove manipulation.
- Buying more tickets increases spending and does not create a positive expected return.
- DrawScope is not financial advice and does not sell tickets.
- Participate only where legal, at age 18 or older in Illinois, and within personal limits.

DrawScope uses “historical analysis,” “observed frequency,” “experimental score,” “simulated result,” “backtested result,” “no demonstrated predictive advantage,” and “consistent with random variation.” It does not use guaranteed-win or predictive claims.
