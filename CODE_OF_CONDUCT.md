# Community expectations

Be respectful, specific, and evidence-driven. Discuss the work rather than the person, protect private information, and avoid claims that historical analysis guarantees future outcomes.

Harassment, discriminatory behavior, deliberate disclosure of private information, and unsafe or deceptive contributions are not accepted. Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content and restrict participation when these expectations are violated.

Report conduct concerns privately through the maintainer's GitHub profile rather than escalating them in a public issue.
